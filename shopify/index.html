  • We start by listening. First we talk about what your shop needs right now. Then we figure out the plan together.

Questions folks ask

Do I still pay Shopify?

Yes. Your Shopify plan is billed by Shopify. Our work is billed separately, by us.

How do you get into my shop?

Through Shopify's collaborator access. We send a request, you approve it with a code from your Shopify admin, and you pick the permissions. You can remove our access whenever you want.

Can you work on my current theme?

Usually, yes. We'd rather improve what's already working than start over.

What does it cost?

Every shop is different, so we talk through what you need first and then give you a quote.
